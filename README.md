# Deep Native Engine

Deep Native Engine helps curious learners, IT practitioners and professionals in other fields learn to use AI in practical ways. You can start without coding or a career plan: choose a direction, try a small exercise and decide what to learn next.

[Explore the product introduction](https://deepnative.github.io/deep-native-engine/) for the current experience, who it serves and how to get started.

## What you can do in the current preview

- **Find a starting point.** Choose whether you want to explore AI, use it at work or build something. Your goal and background shape a simple learning path that you can change later.
- **Learn by doing.** Work through a short lesson on giving AI a clear instruction, then practise with invented examples. Browse sample lessons and compare your own response with the source material. Opt into a private sample practice conversation, revisit up to 15 response/comparison pairs, export your history or withdraw its text. Comparisons are deterministic prompts, not AI or qualified assessment. Expired sessions cannot retrieve your private practice history; see the [practice guide](assets/docs/PRIVATE-PRACTICE-SESSIONS.md) for recovery after an uncertain save.
- **Keep track of your work.** Save an exercise draft, mark it complete through self-assessment, and revisit your private learning activity. Switching goals keeps your earlier practice attached to its original direction. You can also plan milestones and keep sample assignments or evidence. Draft, submit and revise private sample assignments while retaining earlier submissions and self-reflections; [attempt recovery](assets/docs/PRIVATE-ATTEMPT-RECOVERY.md) lets you inspect an unconfirmed action without repeating it. Keep private notes on sample workflow versions; if a save or withdrawal is unconfirmed, reopen the note to check its state before trying again. See [private workflow feedback](assets/docs/CTP-013-PRIVATE-WORKFLOW-FEEDBACK.md). Report whether a sample lesson helped, correct your answer or withdraw it; [usefulness recovery](assets/docs/USEFULNESS-RECOVERY.md) lets you check an unconfirmed action before trying again.
- **Enter your granted local staff work.** When explicitly enabled, sign in through the staff form, discover tools for your current role, and sign out of staff tools while keeping learner access and saved progress. Existing exact grants and expiry still govern each tool; see [local staff entry](assets/docs/PRIVATE-LOCAL-STAFF-ENTRY.md).
- **Assign exact private support work.** In the enabled local administrator browser, check a member-supplied request receipt and an operator's own assignment ID, confirm a finite assignment, inspect its history and revoke it. The operator can then discover the assigned request. No member/staff directory or support minutes are created; see [support assignments](assets/docs/PRIVATE-SUPPORT-ASSIGNMENT.md).
- **Assign exact private sample feedback.** In the enabled local administrator browser, check the sample ID/version displayed to its owner and a reviewer’s own reference, confirm a finite assignment, inspect its receipt and revoke that exact grant. Overlapping access stays separate; uncertain results have explicit saved-state inspection. See [private sample assignments](assets/docs/PRIVATE-SAMPLE-ASSIGNMENTS.md).
- **Offer one exact workflow note for private reading.** Deliberately request finite review of a saved note, have a local administrator assign its exact source to a moderator, and withdraw permission while keeping your note. Corrections, deletion/recreation and grant revocation block earlier access. This permits reading, not a promised reply or qualified assessment; see [private workflow review](assets/docs/PRIVATE-WORKFLOW-REVIEW.md).
- **Exchange private sample feedback.** An explicitly authorized local reviewer can discover assigned samples in an active/completed worklist, then draft source-quoted comments and publish them to the sample owner. Ask one clarification and receive one answer, retain earlier feedback when revising, or withdraw access and delete the sample. Optionally reserve existing review test minutes, explicitly begin review, and publish with actual simulated effort while returning unused minutes; see [review test minutes](assets/docs/PRIVATE-REVIEW-TEST-MINUTES.md). This is human-authored sample feedback, not formal assessment or a paid review; see [the feedback guide](assets/docs/PRIVATE-SAMPLE-FEEDBACK.md) and [reviewer worklist](assets/docs/PRIVATE-REVIEWER-WORKLIST.md).
- **Try private sample support.** Submit an invented request, follow its receipt and read explicitly shared local operator replies. Allocate existing support test minutes, cancel an unstarted hold, and see used and returned minutes after a separately granted local operator records bounded effort. A separately authorized local operator can see the request’s observed age and one exact test-effort receipt together; reading starts no work and grants no extra access. Withdraw request text or export your retained records. Coverage is unverified and no response deadline is promised; see [operator setup](assets/docs/PRIVATE-SAMPLE-SUPPORT.md) and [test-minute guide](assets/docs/PRIVATE-SUPPORT-TEST-MINUTES.md).
- **Explore sample appointment windows.** With explicitly seeded test minutes, reserve a short sample hold, withdraw it and reload your own receipt. Expired sessions cannot retrieve private receipts or allowances, including during a delayed check. These are examples, not real bookings; see [the sample-hold guide](assets/docs/CTP-012-MEMBER-SAMPLE-HOLDS.md).
- **See your local test units.** Inspect your own configured test minutes, sessions and requests, then download a current summary or your retained grant, hold, event and settlement history. Held and expired units stay distinct; history counters are separate from current usable balances. Reading creates no allowance, booking or purchase; see [the private preview guide](assets/docs/PRIVATE-MEMBER-TEST-UNITS.md).
- **Run a local request with test units.** Choose one invented text sample and explicitly hold an existing study request. Completion consumes it once; cancellation before it starts releases it subject to expiry. Uncertain outcomes remain held. See [the local request guide](assets/docs/PRIVATE-LOCAL-AI-TEST-REQUESTS.md).
- **Inspect assigned local request holds.** Explicitly assigned operators can read safe metadata for uncertain local test requests. Private source content stays hidden; inspection never retries or resolves the hold. See [the operator guide](assets/docs/PRIVATE-LOCAL-AI-HOLD-INSPECTION.md).
- **Inspect local synthetic accounting.** Operators can view observed unit balances and structural discrepancies without exposing member details. This does not verify payments or qualified service capacity; see [the report guide](assets/docs/PRIVATE-SYNTHETIC-LEDGER-RECONCILIATION.md).
- **Try invented circle discussion.** In an explicitly enabled local sandbox, separately choose sharing, exchange invented questions and replies, save private sample reports and observe scoped synthetic hide/restore actions. Withdraw your text, leave and export or erase your own records. See [the circle discussion guide](assets/docs/LOCAL-CIRCLE-DISCUSSION.md) for setup and limits.
- **Grant exact local circle moderation.** An enabled local administrator can check a moderator’s own reference, confirm a finite grant for one circle, inspect its history or an uncertain result, and revoke that exact grant. Existing report/hide/restore access remains scoped; overlapping grants stay separate. See [circle moderation grants](assets/docs/PRIVATE-CIRCLE-GRANT-ADMIN.md).
- **Rehearse private event enrollment.** In an explicitly enabled local sandbox, reserve an invented event seat, reload your private receipt, withdraw and download or erase your registration history. Capacity is shared safely between sample members. A selected local administrator can also schedule a dated invented rehearsal from the trusted template; members discover it and use the same registration flow. See [dated rehearsal scheduling](assets/docs/PRIVATE-EVENT-SCHEDULING.md). A selected local administrator can cancel an exact invented event version; members retain cancelled receipts and can still withdraw or erase their own registration. This is not a real appointment. See [event cancellation and recovery](assets/docs/PRIVATE-EVENT-CANCELLATION.md). See [the local registration guide](assets/docs/PRIVATE-EVENT-REGISTRATION.md).
- **Explore ways to participate.** Try local previews of learning circles and events. If joining or leaving is unconfirmed, reopen current membership before acting again; [circle recovery](assets/docs/CIRCLE-MEMBERSHIP-RECOVERY.md) preserves earlier membership history. Draft a private contribution, revise it using explicit moderator feedback, and confirm rights again before resubmitting. If a proposal action is unconfirmed, inspect its current private state before acting again; an unconfirmed creation may already have saved a draft. See [proposal recovery](assets/docs/PROPOSAL-LIFETIME-RECOVERY.md). Optional career planning stays private and unsent.

This is a **local learning preview**, not a launched service. Lessons and participation examples are synthetic. There is no live AI provider, public community, staffed moderation, qualified assessment, real booking or payment flow. Use invented or sample information only. See the [product direction](assets/docs/PRODUCT-DIRECTION.md) for the wider vision and the [project board](https://github.com/orgs/deepnative/projects/1/views/1) for work still in progress.

## Run the local learning preview

Your preview work is stored in local PostgreSQL and accessed through your browser session. Use it on your own computer with sample data.

Prerequisites: Node 24 (`.nvmrc`), Git, Make, Python 3.11+, Docker/Compose with a running daemon.

```sh
make setup
make verify
make dev
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:3000`. Setup installs locked dependencies, Chromium, Firefox and WebKit for browser checks, the local database and the pre-push hook. It preserves an existing `.env`. See [verification and troubleshooting](assets/docs/workflows/VERIFICATION.md).

The [quality gates](assets/docs/context/QUALITY-GATES.md) and [scope and acceptance](assets/docs/INITIAL-LEARNING-SLICE.md) explain what is tested in this preview and what remains for the full MVP.

## Team setup

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the [team workflow](assets/docs/workflows/TEAM-WORKFLOW.md) before taking an issue. Codex discovers the repo-local skills and custom agents when this repository is opened as a trusted project; reload the project after adding them if needed. Use the same written workflow in other assistants.

| Task                         | Skill                | Suggested role                          |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Clarify scope and acceptance | `$dne-plan-issue`    | `dne-planner`                           |
| Deliver an authorized slice  | `$dne-deliver-issue` | `dne-builder` or `dne-critical-builder` |
| Review changes and evidence  | `$dne-review-change` | `dne-reviewer`                          |
| Validate and hand off work   | `$dne-handoff`       | `dne-verifier`                          |

The [documentation index](assets/docs/README.md) includes the original context, model recommendations, templates, and workflow. Shared instructions make the process reproducible; model outputs still require review and tests. Delivery includes merging accepted changes to `main` and verifying the resulting CI.
