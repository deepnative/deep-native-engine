# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to apply AI in everyday life, professional work or technical
projects. The learning foundation does not require coding, an employer or a paid
coaching plan. Everyday, Work and Technical are example routes, not access roles.

## Product Purpose

Deep Native Engine helps learners understand AI, practise with sources they can
check, create useful work and explain their decisions. Learning, application,
participation and contribution are the product direction. A local reader is the
current content and workflow experiment, separate from the hosted application.

## Operating Context

The local reader contains six authored foundation lessons and three substantial
final cases. Learners move from example to practice, a fresh case and their own
work. Final cases retain the first draft, changed source information and revision.
The owner currently prioritizes content creation/refinement, realistic examples,
user behavior and BDD workflows; production work remains parked.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing native JavaScript, HTML and CSS reader; authored Markdown generates
  versioned course data. Preserve source identity and exact notebook versions.
- Explicit browser save and text download; separate route drafts, earlier-notebook
  recovery and failure handling. No automatic save or cross-device account sync.
- Deterministic choice feedback, supplied examples and learner self-checks are
  different evidence. Free writing is not automatically graded.
- Practice feedback/contribution records are private local exercises; no reviewer
  is contacted and no proposal is publicly submitted.
- No live AI provider, paid service, manufactured classmates, streak, mastery,
  credential or efficacy claim is part of this redesign.
- Runtime remains provider-free and preserves authored content and all existing
  functional tests. Local evidence is not production acceptance.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Deep Native name. The owner requested a GUI combining the best features
of Duolingo, Quantic EMBA learning and Khan Academy. Use their learning interaction
patterns as references; create one coherent Deep Native interface, retaining our
own content, identity and truthful progress. Routine design decisions are delegated.

## Evidence on Hand

`assets/docs/learning-design/lessons/`, three realistic final-practice packets,
the reader's source-bound feedback and contribution examples, `learning.feature`,
and executable browser checks. The owner reported no confusing step in the most
recent final-exercise check; that is feedback, not an independent efficacy study.
The October 28 technical audit recorded focus, readability, mobile wayfinding and
heading issues in the incumbent interface.

## Product Principles

1. Let learners make a decision, see its consequence and try again.
2. Keep source evidence close to the question or draft it supports.
3. Show a useful next action and progress based on actual work.
4. Preserve private writing and version history through navigation and recovery.
5. Make behavior reviewable through BDD and runnable local evidence.
