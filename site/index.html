01 / READ THE SAMPLE

Give AI a clear task.

A useful request names the goal, supplies the details, and explains what to check. Missing details should stay questions, not become invented facts.

Invented workshop brief

A neighbourhood group is holding a beginner-friendly paper-craft workshop on Saturday, 2–3 pm, at the sample Willow Room. Materials are supplied. Capacity and accessibility arrangements have not been confirmed.

Write an instruction asking for a friendly invitation of no more than 80 words. Include the known details and ask the writer to flag missing information.

See an example instruction

Draft a friendly invitation of at most 80 words for the paper-craft workshop using only the brief above. Do not guess the capacity or accessibility arrangements; list those as questions for the organiser. Check the time and place against the brief.

06 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful
answers.

Still wondering about the preview? Write to DN Team.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. The first learning experience is made for beginners and uses everyday, invented examples. Technical paths are optional.

Can I use the app online right now?+

Yes. Open the existing user portal for the Azure application and its saved sample work. The exercise on this page is a separate, self-contained walkthrough. Its simulated actions stay in this browser tab and do not create or change a portal account.

Is there a live AI tutor or formal assessment?+

No. The preview uses sample content and deterministic exercises. It does not connect to a live AI provider or offer qualified assessment.

What does it cost?+

Foundation access and pricing have not been decided. These previews have no payment flow, and no coaching package is required to explore the learning experience.
