# Private support test minutes

Issue [#441](https://github.com/deepnative/deep-native-engine/issues/441) adds a private, invented-data support effort flow. Use the linked issue for the current acceptance, exact-revision verification, main CI and milestone-demo evidence. It is not live staffing, a purchased allowance, real cost accounting, an SLA, qualified review or full-MVP acceptance.

## Member journey

Open an owned private sample support request. Enter and explicitly confirm a whole-minute ceiling from 1 through 120. One current owned `support_minutes` test allowance must cover the entire ceiling; selection uses earliest expiry and then stable identifier. The flow creates no units, combines no grants and transfers nothing. The saved receipt shows held, consumed and released quantities. An exact original allocation key replays its saved outcome; changing its ceiling conflicts. One unresolved allocation per request prevents a second hold.

Before begin, explicitly cancel the unstarted allocation to release it once and retain the request. A later allocation needs a fresh confirmation and key. Withdrawal before begin also releases the hold and removes request text/messages. After begin, cancellation is refused; withdrawal or revocation of the bound time grant retains the unresolved hold for reconciliation. Never invent an entry or automatically refund or retry that work.

## Operator journey and protected local instructions

The existing `support-request-local-v1` note/reply grant is independent and unmetered. It grants no time or debit permission. A `support-time-local-v1` grant binds a current staff role to one request and one allocation. That time grant exposes a content-free effort receipt, not request text or internal notes. Learner background, goals and paid-plan assumptions grant no staff privileges.

Use the existing protected `support:local-admin` setup described in [private sample support](PRIVATE-SAMPLE-SUPPORT.md). Keep private instruction files in its private `support-admin` directory with mode 0600. A `time-grant` instruction contains exactly `requestId`, `allocationId`, `idempotencyKey`, `startsAt` and `expiresAt`; dates are finite UTC timestamps. The CLI reads the protected operator identity and platform administrator credentials itself. Run `npm run support:local-admin -- time-grant instruction.json`. A separate `time-revoke` instruction contains only `grantId`; run `npm run support:local-admin -- time-revoke instruction.json`. Never put tokens, credentials or private instruction files in the repository, screenshots or public links. Existing `grant` and `revoke` commands remain for note/reply grants.

Open `/operator/support-time` in the current authorized local operator session. It lists at most 20 exact current grants per page. Opening a receipt records no begin or charge. Confirm begin separately; one durable actor and grant win. Record a mandatory support interval and optional preparation interval using canonical UTC timestamps with milliseconds and `Z`. Leave both preparation fields blank when unused. Each duration is a positive whole number of minutes. Total support plus preparation is bounded by the confirmed ceiling; starts must lie within the previous 24 hours, and ends cannot be in the future. Intervals cannot overlap each other or another retained entry by that operator across members/workspaces; half-open adjacent intervals are allowed.

Recording 10 support minutes plus 5 preparation minutes against a 20-minute hold consumes 15 one-minute reservations and releases 5 atomically. Expired unused minutes become expired, never usable. Previously begun work may settle after budget expiry while the current exact staff grant, member and workspace permissions remain valid. An authorized exact stored completion replay precedes a new interval-window check and does not charge twice. Changed payload conflicts. Replies, acknowledgements and resolution remain separate actions.

## Accounting, export and erasure

Migration 054 groups immutable one-minute reservations with owned allocation and entry records. Shared ledger fingerprints and balance operations remain authoritative; generic, AI-job and formal-completion operations cannot settle a support-linked reservation. New requests and historical note grants are not backfilled with time privileges or fabricated effort.

Structural reconciliation validates reservations and support groups before category aggregation. Each support entry counts once, and each consumed reservation attaches once. Its support and preparation durations coexist with formal synthetic completions without join fanout. Invalid groups or event history remain separate discrepancies; totals cannot cancel them out. The report exposes aggregates, not private accounting identifiers or costs.

Structured export `local-member-records-v16` appends `supportTimeAllocations` and `supportTimeEntries` after the existing support sections, retaining cursor v2 positions for older sections and the 100-record/256-KiB page bounds. Allocation exports include owned request/group references, fixed policy, ceiling/state/dates, synthetic attribution and held/consumed/released counts. Entries include owned request/group references, interval timestamps, support/preparation counts, creation time and synthetic attribution. They omit ledger reservations/events/grants, fingerprints, idempotency keys, staff identities/tokens and internal notes. Withdrawal retains only the existing redacted request receipt and content-free effort history. Member erasure cascades that owner's accounting and time records without refunding an orphan hold or changing another owner's balances, retained entries or overlap protection.

## Pause and rollback

Set `DNE_SUPPORT_TIME_WRITES=disabled` and restart **all compatible application and local-admin processes**. This disables allocation, cancellation, time-grant/revocation, begin and record writes. Reads/export remain available; operator mutation controls are hidden. Existing request text withdrawal and member erasure remain available under their current privacy authorization. An explicit withdrawal still releases an unstarted hold or retains begun work, using the preserved database guards; it is not a bulk rollback refund.

Stop older writers and migration runners. Do not run an older binary that ignores the switch, replays migration 053's release implementation, omits support export records or lacks current protected-operation checks. Preserve migration 054, all tables, immutable history, holds, constraints/triggers and the shared release body. Do not drop tables, run a down migration, remove links, automatically release unresolved work or rerun uncertain actions. Roll back the affected application writes only to a compatible implementation that preserves these invariants and the v16 export contract. Re-enable with `DNE_SUPPORT_TIME_WRITES=enabled` only after the compatible exact revision and affected gates pass.

If COMMIT or connection handback cannot be confirmed, withhold success. Inspect saved state in a fresh authorized session; a known stored exact-key replay is safe. Absence or an unresolved begun hold is not permission to fabricate completion, pick a new allocation or rerun automatically. Record the uncertainty and preserve the original key and attempted intervals privately.

Delivery evidence must include the real PostgreSQL race/fault/expiry/migration/erasure cases, maximum 120-minute operation bound, all-source unit metrics and approved browser matrix. A focused check is not the complete gate. After verified main, demonstrate the actual invented-data member/operator journey with its revision, provider state, access/restart instructions and limitations.

## Local operation limits

The application caps each group at 120 one-minute reservations, uses five-second lock and statement timeouts, and checks a ten-second aggregate operation deadline before commit. The maximum-size local API test bounds allocation and partial settlement to 1,600 application-issued queries each in one transaction; database trigger work is inside that transaction and included in elapsed time, not counted as separate client queries. Migration 054 takes table/constraint locks during its additive transaction; stop compatible writers before an operational migration and preserve rollback state. Local invented-data measurements do not prove production latency or staffing capacity.

## Connected request and effort view

Issue [#445](https://github.com/deepnative/deep-native-engine/issues/445) connects the existing operator request worklist/detail to one independently authorized effort receipt. Every request page shows its received timestamp, one common observed UTC instant, nonnegative elapsed calendar seconds, current request grant window and currently allowed request actions. Calendar age is not a staffed, business-day or holiday-adjusted SLA. A resolved request has no remaining request actions.

Request authority alone reveals no allocation identifier, quantities, time-grant existence or effort link. Missing, not-started, expired, revoked or foreign effort permission has the same “No separately authorized test effort shown” state. When both exact purposes are current for the actor's current role and active source, the most recently created current time grant supplies one allocation-specific receipt; equal creation instants use descending grant identifier. This is not an aggregate across allocations, purchased allowance or available expert capacity. Time authority alone still exposes no request text. Follow the named receipt link to separately confirm begin or record using the unchanged effort workflow. Reading either page performs none of those mutations.

The combined read uses one transaction: bounded request/effort candidate discovery, sorted active principals, current staff profile, exact request grants, selected time grants, owned workspaces, requests, allocations, then a final database-clock lifetime fence before COMMIT/connection handback. Revocation, source removal and effort transitions serialize against those locks. A discovered association changing before authorization withholds the whole response; it does not backfill another grant. A fresh read independently checks both authorities. Uncertain COMMIT or handback withholds success and never retries automatically.

Keyset pages retain 20 displayed requests plus one sentinel and at most one effort candidate per selected request. SQL and lock deadlines remain five seconds and the complete connected operation remains bounded by ten seconds, including connection acquisition, commit and handback. A client-side per-query timer also covers a withheld driver reply before server timeouts are configured or after COMMIT; its budget is capped by the remaining total deadline. A timed-out connection is discarded without queuing rollback or retrying uncertain work. The measured invented-data fixture spans 106 requests, 22 maximum 120-minute allocations and six pages; actual candidate and selected request/grant/allocation lock input bounds and PostgreSQL EXPLAIN evidence are recorded on the issue. Existing indexes suffice for this measured fixture; no migration, historical backfill or quantity change is introduced. These local measurements are not production latency guarantees.

`DNE_SUPPORT_TIME_WRITES=disabled` keeps currently authorized connected links and content-free receipts readable while the independent receipt hides begin/record controls. Roll back this additive read/view change only to the previous compatible support-time application revision while preserving migration 054, time-write pause behavior, protected grants, retained allocations/entries, export/deletion contracts and immutable accounting. Never restore removed text or drop support tables. Keep the current implementation if an older reader would omit required member records.
