# CTP-022 local preview metrics slice

The operator-only `GET /operator/metrics` endpoint returns one aggregate snapshot from the local preview database. It accepts only an active, unrevoked `operator` or `platform_admin` staff principal. It never returns member IDs, profiles, evidence or per-member drill-down. Every response is labelled `synthetic-local-preview`; none is a live business KPI. The timestamp is the database statement time. No external analytics provider receives these data.

The fixed denominator for every rate is the number of **retained** learner rows at that timestamp, including members whose sessions expired or were revoked. A privacy deletion removes the learner and all linked events; the snapshot cannot reconstruct prior deleted members, so it is not a durable acquisition cohort. Zero members means the rate is undefined, not zero success. The endpoint supplies counts and definitions, not a rounded or potentially misleading rate.

- Activation counts each member with at least one versioned lesson open once. Opening is observed but does not establish learning.
- Completion counts each member with at least one self-assessed lesson completion once. It is member reported, not a formal assessment or observed skill.
- Participation counts each member who has ever joined a synthetic local circle once. Leaving does not erase this history or remove the member from its denominator.
- Active circle count includes each member with at least one circle membership that has no `left_at`; it is a state count, not participation volume or expert attendance.
- Observed assignment submission counts each retained member with at least one immutable private assignment submission snapshot once. Revisions do not increase the member count. This is a submitted response, not reviewed project quality, verified completion or skill.
- Observed cross-content return uses the member's earliest retained versioned lesson open as its anchor. The denominator is retained members whose anchor is at least 14 full days old at the same statement time. The numerator is those members with a different lesson content ID's first open or an assignment submission at or after day 7 and before day 14 from the anchor. A repeat view or newer version of the anchor lesson does not count as cross-content return. This bounded proxy does not measure all learning retention. A zero denominator makes a rate undefined; the endpoint returns counts rather than a rate.

Counts are computed from the same database statement, avoiding mixed-time totals. The aggregate intentionally has no small-segment or individual filters. Calendar enrollment cohorts, overlapping background/goal segmentation, broad continued learning, reviewed contribution quality, usefulness, paid conversion/renewal, ledger/provider reconciliation, service cost and capacity are not represented. The first-open proxy cannot reconstruct a deleted member or deleted attempt; no post-deletion analytics record is kept. Those broader measures require their own event semantics, privacy review, source readiness and evidence before a full-MVP metric or public report can claim them. The outstanding ECO-08 full-MVP journey remains open.

The approved local browser scenario L47 checks operator access, member denial, synthetic labelling and count changes after onboarding and joining. L68 checks the retained-record submission and cross-content return aggregates and member denial. L47/L68 cover only the synthetic learning subset of ECO-08-A and operator/member denial subset of ECO-08-C; they do not supply contribution, paid, live-demo exclusion or full-release browser evidence for ECO-08. Real PostgreSQL integration tests check duplicate-safe counting, date-window edges, attempt/account deletion, a departed circle member, an expired member in the denominator, and staff revocation. The code rollback is a revert with no schema migration, but would remove these new measures and their definitions. This slice does not qualify curriculum, launch services or indicate real demand.
