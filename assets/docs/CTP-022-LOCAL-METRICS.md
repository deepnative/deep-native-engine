# CTP-022 local preview metrics slice

## Bands, segments and cohorts — #649, #619

No exact learning count leaves the store. `src/learning-metrics.ts` turns the aggregate into coarse bands (`5-19`, `20-49`, `50-99`, `100-199`, `200+`) before anything reaches the JSON or the report:

- **Small groups.** Nothing is shown below 20 retained members. A value below five, or within five of its denominator, is suppressed. A suppressed value may be zero or not. On a small preview the learning section is therefore fully suppressed until 20 members exist.
- **Segments.** The same measures for each current starting point, current goal and interest tag. Interest tags overlap, so rows can add up to more than the total. A segment is suppressed below 20 members or with fewer than five members outside it, and every known segment is always listed. A segment never implies a paid tier, career path or staff right.
- **Join-month cohorts.** Retained members by UTC join month, latest 12 months, measured over fixed windows from each member's own join time: lesson opened and self-assessed (self-reported) within 14 days, assignment submitted within 30 days, continued from day 7 up to day 30. Later events never count. A cohort shows no values until 30 days after its month ends.

**Retention.** The owner direction of 27 October on #121 applies: retained-account metrics only, with deleted accounts excluded. Deleting an account removes it from its cohort and segments, so denominators shrink and earlier months can change. Historical deleted accounts cannot be reconstructed, and `created_at` for accounts older than migration 003 is a backfill rather than a true join date.

**No new tracking.** This uses existing records only, with no new table, event or provider. Exact counts remain in `exactMetrics`, which no route may serve; database tests use it to check the SQL.

**Limits.** Repeated snapshots near a band boundary can still allow inference, so this is an interim safeguard, not an anonymity claim. Segments use current choices, not values at the time of each event, and demo records are not yet excluded. Both stay on #121.

**Evidence.** MET-SUPP-01, MET-SEG-01/02 and MET-COH-01/02/03 are in `tests/unit/learning-metrics.test.ts` and `tests/integration/learning-cohorts.test.ts`, which failed before implementation. Journeys L222 and L223 read the bands in the browser on desktop and mobile; L47 and L68 confirm no exact count is served.

## Member-reported career outcomes — #123

The snapshot also counts **retained members who reported an outcome on their own plan**: at least one of their private career, opportunity or contract entries has a non-blank self-reported outcome. It counts members, not entries, uses the same bands and suppression, and appears in every segment row as "Reported an outcome". The report labels it member-reported and not verified. It is not a hiring, placement, contract or income rate, and no outcome text, entry or member is read into the report. Plans without an outcome don't count. A deleted account leaves the count with its entries.

Demo records are still not separated, so this is no live outcome figure. Evidence: OUTCOME-01..03 in `tests/integration/career-outcome-metric.test.ts`, and the report wording in `tests/unit/metrics-report.test.ts`.

## Readable staff report

Sign in through the existing staff entry form as a current operator or platform administrator, then open **Learning and participation snapshot** from staff tools. `/operator/metrics/report` renders the existing aggregate as readable HTML with its snapshot time, counts, denominators, plain definitions, return window and usefulness suppression. **Reload snapshot** makes a deliberate new read; **View aggregate JSON** opens the unchanged `/operator/metrics` API. No new tracking, calculation, record, export or staff authority is introduced.

The report preserves the synthetic-local-preview scope even when accessed on a hosted preview. It is not a live business KPI or an assertion that demo records have been separated from ordinary members. Existing aggregate counts are distinct retained members; overlapping measures must not be added together. Zero retained/eligible members make a rate undefined. The completion count covers versioned library lessons, not the separate starter exercise. The return proxy is neither an enrollment cohort nor general retention. Deleted accounts are excluded, while retained expired/revoked member sessions and earlier circle participation remain under the existing definitions.

Members, unrelated staff roles, expired/revoked staff and signed-out staff selection cannot read the report. It rejects member/lesson/segment/date query parameters, offers no individual drill-down, sends `no-store`, and displays no partial count or internal diagnostic on database failure. After recovery, a deliberate reload can read a fresh snapshot. The original JSON query and authorization contract is unchanged. The existing exact aggregate counts and coarse usefulness disclosure are reused; this presentation does not establish a new production disclosure policy or remove repeated-snapshot inference risks.

## Existing aggregate contract

The operator-only `GET /operator/metrics` endpoint returns one aggregate snapshot from the local preview database. It accepts only an active, unrevoked `operator` or `platform_admin` staff principal. It never returns member IDs, profiles, evidence or per-member drill-down. Every response is labelled `synthetic-local-preview`; none is a live business KPI. Each JSON download writes a `staff_export_events` row saying who took it and when, before the data is sent; if that write fails, no data is sent. The on-screen report isn't logged. The timestamp is the database statement time. No external analytics provider receives these data.

The fixed denominator for every rate is the number of **retained** learner rows at that timestamp, including members whose sessions expired or were revoked. A privacy deletion removes the learner and all linked events; the snapshot cannot reconstruct prior deleted members, so it is not a durable acquisition cohort. Zero members means the rate is undefined, not zero success. The endpoint supplies counts and definitions, not a rounded or potentially misleading rate.

- Activation counts each member with at least one versioned lesson open once. Opening is observed but does not establish learning.
- Completion counts each member with at least one self-assessed lesson completion once. It is member reported, not a formal assessment or observed skill.
- Participation counts each member who has ever joined a synthetic local circle once. Leaving does not erase this history or remove the member from its denominator.
- Active circle count includes each member with at least one circle membership that has no `left_at`; it is a state count, not participation volume or expert attendance.
- Observed assignment submission counts each retained member with at least one immutable private assignment submission snapshot once. Revisions do not increase the member count. This is a submitted response, not reviewed project quality, verified completion or skill.
- Observed cross-content return uses the member's earliest retained versioned lesson open as its anchor. The denominator is retained members whose anchor is at least 14 full days old at the same statement time. The numerator is those members with a different lesson content ID's first open or an assignment submission at or after day 7 and before day 14 from the anchor. A repeat view or newer version of the anchor lesson does not count as cross-content return. This bounded proxy does not measure all learning retention. A zero denominator makes a rate undefined; the endpoint returns counts rather than a rate.
- Member-reported usefulness is a separate coarse disclosure, not a count or learning score. Among retained members with at least one private exact-version report, only the most recently updated current answer per member contributes, with a deterministic version/content tie order. Correcting or withdrawing changes that member's contribution; deletion removes it. A historical lesson answer remains a report of that earlier version, not current curriculum approval. The response denominator is distinct respondents, not all retained members, lesson opens or enrollment cohorts. The endpoint returns no exact respondent or choice counts, member IDs, lesson IDs/versions, raw answers or per-version drill-down. With fewer than 20 respondents or fewer than five in either choice, the entire usefulness block is `suppressed`. Otherwise it returns only a broad helpful-share band of 0–24%, 25–49%, 50–74% or 75–100%. Caller-supplied query parameters cannot select a member, content version or segment. Background and goal labels do not change access or grouping.

The usefulness threshold and coarsening are **interim synthetic-preview safeguards**, not an approved production disclosure policy. Repeated snapshots, especially near a threshold or band boundary, may still support inference; a qualified privacy review and production query policy remain open. The only persisted source is the member-owned `lesson_usefulness` row that cascades on deletion. There is no operator analytics table, cache or external export. Database failure yields a generic 503 rather than partial counts. The snapshot timestamp and existing aggregate counts come from one database statement.

Counts are computed from the same database statement, avoiding mixed-time totals. The aggregate intentionally has no small-segment or individual filters. Calendar enrollment cohorts, overlapping background/goal segmentation, broad continued learning, the instructional quality of contributions (#122 adds only event volumes and an editorial-outcome share; see [contribution metrics](CONTRIBUTION-METRICS.md)), live usefulness, paid conversion/renewal, ledger/provider reconciliation, service cost and capacity are not represented. The first-open proxy cannot reconstruct a deleted member or deleted attempt; no post-deletion analytics record is kept. Those broader measures require their own event semantics, privacy review, source readiness and evidence before a full-MVP metric or public report can claim them. The outstanding ECO-08 full-MVP journey remains open.
