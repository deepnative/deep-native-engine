## Review and publication gates

1. **Content owner:** Tom Wu must accept the learning goals, audience fit, limitations, rights inventory and release scope. The front matter's owner value records interim accountability, not approval.
2. **Qualified review:** name and verify reviewers with relevant QA and nontechnical workflow expertise; review assignment reference outputs, rubrics and calibration samples. The [PLAN-003 roster gate](PLAN-003-CAPACITY-OPERATIONS.md) remains unconfirmed. No review minutes, booking or sale are inferred from this draft.
3. **Accessibility:** conduct human reading and keyboard/screen-reader review on the rendered application, check heading order, plain language, focus and nonvisual instructions with applicable audiences. Markdown text alone is not proof of application accessibility.
4. **Rights and privacy:** confirm sources/ownership and that all retained examples are synthetic. Member contributions need separate attribution, destination consent, moderation, revocation and publication review before the application can show them as approved instruction.
5. **Version and refresh:** publish only an explicitly approved immutable version through CTP-007's future workflow. A changed rubric creates a new version; it never rewrites a completed review. After publication, slower-moving lessons have a proposed quarterly review and workflow demonstrations a proposed monthly/dependency-change compatibility check, following the archived content-operations target. A missed check removes any claim of current compatibility.

### Calibration boundary

The two assignment files include a reference response, criterion-level rubric and two simulated scorer notes each. These are **draft calibration prompts**, not evidence that a qualified reviewer agreed. A future calibration session should have at least two authorized reviewers independently score the same synthetic samples, record criterion disagreements and reasons, revise the rubric version if needed, and sign the accepted interpretation with date and specialty. Formal feedback must bind to the submitted work, assignment/rubric version and assigned human reviewer; preparation time fits a funded allowance if a paid service is later approved.
