## Read or rehearse the pack

The six text lessons form a complete practice sequence for general learners, non-IT professionals and IT practitioners. No coding, job, client, paid coaching plan or AI account is required. Read the text, choose an invented example, write a response and compare it with the self-check. Text is the original teaching format, so no video transcript is needed. An editorial readability check does not establish screen-reader or release accessibility acceptance.
