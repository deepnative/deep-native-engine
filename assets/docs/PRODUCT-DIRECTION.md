# Deep Native Engine: AI learning and participation ecosystem

Decision date: 21 September 2026. The owner broadened the audience from individual contractors to IT practitioners, professionals in other fields, and other people who want to learn and take part in the future of AI. This is the current product direction. Application implementation remains on hold.

**Purpose:** help people understand AI, use it in meaningful work and everyday projects, build practical skills, and participate in a community where they can learn with others and contribute what they create.

## Who it serves

| Audience | Example goals | Suitable starting experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IT practitioners | Apply AI to engineering, QA, data, security, operations or analysis | Technical workflows and role-specific practice |
| Professionals in other fields | Improve research, communication, planning, creative or operational work | Accessible examples using their own domain and approved synthetic data |
| Students, career changers and early-career learners | Build AI literacy, explore directions and demonstrate skills | Guided foundation, exploratory projects and optional career practice |
| Creators, entrepreneurs and independent professionals | Build useful projects, workflows or services | Applied projects, peer learning and optional business coaching |
| Curious and lifelong learners | Understand AI, use it responsibly and participate with others | A beginner-friendly foundation without coding or employment prerequisites |

These are overlapping backgrounds and interests, not admission barriers or access-control roles. A member may select several backgrounds, change goals, or choose an exploratory starting point. The original nine IT role families remain optional technical specializations. Contractors remain welcome as one audience; a client, contract, employer, job title or income claim is never required to start the common learning journey.

## Shared product foundation

1. **Discover and orient:** explain the ecosystem, available learning paths, participation options and actual readiness. Capture learning goals, experience, interests, accessibility needs and available time without requiring commercial information.
2. **Learn and practise:** provide accessible AI foundations, domain-relevant examples, guided exercises, study support and progress that reflects real activity. Beginner and noncoding paths must be complete experiences.
3. **Apply and demonstrate:** let members complete practical projects or workflows, receive appropriately labelled feedback and maintain evidence of their learning. Self-reflection, peer feedback, AI suggestions and qualified formal review are distinct.
4. **Participate:** let members opt into interest groups, learning circles, clinics or project discussions, ask questions, help peers and leave a group. Provide community rules, reporting and moderation ownership for shared spaces.
5. **Contribute:** let members propose examples, reusable workflows or learning resources with attribution, rights and a review/publication lifecycle. Sharing is explicit; private assignments do not become public by joining a group. Recognition must describe observed contributions rather than imply unearned credentials.

Participation starts with bounded cohort spaces, learning events and reviewed contributions. A public social network, job marketplace, organization billing and unrestricted hosted agents remain separate proposals under the existing expansion gates. These exclusions do not defer non-IT learners or core ecosystem participation to Phase F.

## Optional paths and commercial assumptions

Learning and participation are the common product. Career preparation, interviews, independent consulting, contract renewal and high-touch coaching are optional paths selected by the member. Professional background must not be confused with the legacy offer named **Professional** or with privileged staff permissions.

The original CAD 3,000 pilot, explicit CAD 9,000 continuation, CAD 12,000 annual Professional coaching package, and higher-tier service quotas remain versioned **optional coaching-offer hypotheses**. They are not universal membership prices or prerequisites for joining the learning ecosystem. The original standalone-pilot, anniversary, explicit purchase, cancellation and ledger invariants still apply whenever those offers are tested or sold.

PLAN-001 and CTP-002 must define and review the foundation/participation access model separately, including any prices, limits or subsidies. No free tier, new price, live entitlement or new revenue forecast is assumed by this refactor. Revalidate each audience's needs, willingness to pay and delivery costs before making paid promises. Expert capacity gates tailored paid services; they do not require every learner to buy coaching or wait for an IT-specialist admission review.

## Delivery and evidence

- Phase A defines the cross-audience story map, shared curriculum, learner goals, participation rules, access model and qualified service boundaries.
- B1 demonstrates onboarding, an appropriate lesson/exercise, private progress and the existing review path for IT, non-IT professional and general-learner examples. It clearly previews participation options without pretending unfinished features work.
- B2 delivers the bounded participation/contribution flows and the remaining private-MVP capabilities, including optional paid-service workflows. Broad audiences and participation are part of this scope.
- B3 verifies the complete approved journey register, including the [audience and participation additions](context/ECOSYSTEM-JOURNEYS.md), privacy and commercial invariants.
- C/D test the optional coached pilot and continuation with actual buyers; E tests repeatable learning usefulness, participation and service delivery. F evaluates separately approved expansions.

Success evidence includes useful exercise/project completion, continued learning, helpful participation, reviewed contributions and accessibility across learner backgrounds. Paid conversion, coaching delivery cost and member-reported career/commercial outcomes are separate measures with explicit denominators. Attendance, generated text or a model forecast does not prove learning, employment or income.

The existing quality contract remains >=99% for every unit metric, >=99% documented E2E journeys, 100% critical journeys and required test passes. The broader scope adds acceptance scenarios; it does not remove difficult old cases or lower thresholds. No application coverage has been measured yet.
