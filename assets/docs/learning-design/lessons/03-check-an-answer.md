# 3. Check an answer against its source

**You will leave with:** a corrected answer and a short explanation of what you changed. Use a source below or the invented source you kept from Lesson 2. Allow about 12–15 minutes; no AI account is needed.

## See it

An answer can sound fluent while mixing accurate facts, mistakes and guesses. Check one claim at a time:

- **Supported:** the source states it, or the conclusion follows directly from the supplied facts.
- **Contradicted:** the source gives an incompatible fact.
- **Not given:** the source does not establish the claim. It could be true elsewhere; you cannot use this source to confirm it.

“Not given” does not mean false. It means you should leave the detail open or find evidence before treating it as a fact.

### Everyday worked check

**Source E3:** Twelve volunteers are available for two hours on Saturday. The venue and budget are undecided. No start time is given.

**Draft answer:** “Fifteen volunteers will meet at the library on Saturday for two hours, starting at 9 a.m.”

| Atomic claim | Status | Why / correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fifteen volunteers | Contradicted | The source says twelve. Change the number. |
| At the library | Not given | The venue is undecided. Say “venue to be confirmed.” |
| On Saturday | Supported | Saturday is explicit. Keep it. |
| For two hours | Supported | The duration is explicit. Keep it. |
| Starting at 9 a.m. | Not given | No start time is supplied. Leave it open. |

**Repaired answer:** “Twelve volunteers are available for two hours on Saturday. The venue, budget and start time are to be confirmed.”

Notice how one sentence needed five separate checks. “This whole sentence is wrong” would lose the useful details as well as the mistakes.

### Work worked check

**Source W3:** The checklist's assigned owner is Sam. Alex gathers feedback. The process test is next Tuesday. No announcement owner or date is recorded.

**Draft answer:** “Alex is the assigned checklist owner and will announce results on Friday.”

“Alex is the assigned checklist owner” conflicts with the assigned owner Sam. Alex announcing results and the Friday date are not given. A repair is: “Sam is the checklist owner. Alex gathers feedback. Announcement ownership and timing are not specified.” The correction does not decide who should announce it.

### Technical worked check

**Source T3:** The sign-up form must explain invalid input without revealing whether an email already has an account. No tests have been run.

**Draft answer:** “The form should protect account privacy. It passed all tests in Firefox.”

The privacy requirement is supported. “It passed all tests” conflicts with “no tests have been run.” Firefox coverage is not given. A repair is: “The form is required to protect account privacy. Test execution status: not run.” This says nothing yet about whether the implementation meets the rule.

## Try a fresh case

Classify the two claims in your route, then rewrite them without an unsupported assertion.

| Case | Fresh source | Claims to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `L3-E-X` | A reading group has nine people and meets for forty-five minutes on Wednesday. A room has not been chosen. | “The group has twelve people.” “The group meets in Room 4.” |
| `L3-W-X` | The agenda's assigned owner is Noor. Lee will invite participants. The meeting is Thursday. No agenda deadline is recorded. | “Lee is the assigned agenda owner.” “The agenda is due Wednesday.” |
| `L3-T-X` | A file form must accept PNG images up to 4 MB. No tests have been run. Its behavior for JPEG is unspecified. | “The form passed its PNG test.” “JPEG uploads must be accepted.” |

<details><summary>Compare your reasoning</summary>

- **Everyday:** Twelve conflicts with nine; Room 4 is not given. Repair: “Nine people will meet for forty-five minutes on Wednesday; the room is to be confirmed.”
- **Work:** Lee as assigned agenda owner conflicts with Noor; Wednesday as an agenda deadline is not given. Repair: “Noor is the agenda owner; its deadline is not specified.”
- **Technical:** A passed PNG test conflicts with no execution; JPEG acceptance is not specified. Repair: “PNG acceptance up to 4 MB is required but not yet tested. The JPEG rule needs clarification.”

If your repair simply deletes everything, restore the facts the source does support. A useful check preserves information as well as removing mistakes.

</details>

## Check your own draft

Use an invented answer to your Lesson 2 request, or deliberately introduce one changed fact and one missing detail into a sample above. No external tool call is necessary.

Create a small table: **claim / source evidence / status / correction or next check**. Then write the corrected answer and finish: “I changed … because ….”

**Self-check rubric:**

| Criterion | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Separate claims | A mixed sentence is split where facts could have different statuses. |
| Classify accurately | Each supported/contradicted claim has source evidence; each not-given claim names what is missing. |
| Repair usefully | Correct facts remain; incompatible facts change; unknown facts stay visibly unknown. |
| Explain a revision | You connect at least one change to source evidence rather than “it sounds better.” |

Mark each criterion **shown** or **needs another try** and keep the explanation. This is your self-check, not a certificate or an automatic grade.

**Take forward:** your source, request, claim table and corrected draft are one small learning artifact. In [Lesson 4: Choose what to use and share](04-choose-what-to-share.md), decide which information to use and who may see it before asking another person for feedback.
