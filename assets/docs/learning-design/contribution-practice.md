# Turn a useful workflow into a contribution

You have a workflow you can explain. Help another learner use it by naming the problem it solves, the change you propose and the evidence behind it. This optional practice uses an original fictional example. Your draft stays in this browser until you explicitly save or download it; no proposal is submitted or shared here.

## Source for this practice

**WF-LAB-01 · version 1 — Make a workshop action list.**

**Invented brief:** Nora drafts invitations. Ivo checks the poster. A small trial is planned for Friday. The invitation deadline, poster-review deadline and trial owner are not specified. The output is for the organising team's planning discussion.

**Workflow v1:** retain the brief → draft task/owner/date rows → copy the trial date into missing deadline cells → call the table verified when all date cells are filled → if the source is unavailable, use last week's plan.

**Output produced by that workflow:** “Nora's invitations are due Friday. Ivo's poster review is due Friday. Nora owns Friday's trial.”

The trial date is supported. Both task deadlines and the trial owner are not given. A complete-looking table can still invent commitments. Last week's plan is not evidence for this week's arrangements.

## Work through a contribution

### First proposal · P1

**Title:** Keep dates attached to their source.

**Problem:** Borrowing Friday from the trial assigns two deadlines that WF-LAB-01 v1 does not establish. Assigning Nora to the trial also goes beyond her invitation task.

**Proposed workflow:** retain the brief and its version → draft task, owner and source-date columns → trace every populated cell to its own statement → retain known facts and write “not specified” for missing commitments → if the source cannot be recovered, keep the draft unverified and ask for the missing owner/date. Do not fill from last week's plan.

**Check:** the repaired table keeps Nora with invitations and Ivo with poster review. Their deadlines stay not specified; Friday stays on the trial and its owner stays not specified. A reader can check every claim against the brief.

**Limits:** this repairs the stated source mismatch. It does not prove the workflow is efficient, that the team agreed a schedule or that a real workshop succeeded.

**Sources and rights note:** original invented proposal and fictional brief for this exercise, referencing WF-LAB-01 v1. No private learner submission, customer record or copied third-party workflow is included. Naming a source alone would not establish permission to reuse it.

### Supplied editorial response to P1

“Your source check fixes the invented deadlines. The team may still want scheduling suggestions. Show how a useful proposed date can remain separate from a commitment, and give an example someone can compare.”

This is authored teaching feedback on P1. It is not a review of the words in your notebook.

### Revised proposal · P2

Keep P1's source check and recovery. Add a **Proposed date** column beside **Source date**, with a **Basis / decision needed** column. Mark a suggestion explicitly and leave the source fact unchanged:

| Task              | Owner from source | Source date   | Proposed date        | Basis / decision needed                                        |
| ----------------- | ----------------- | ------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft invitations | Nora              | Not specified | Tuesday — suggestion | Ask the team whether this would leave enough preparation time. |
| Check poster      | Ivo               | Not specified | None proposed        | Ask when the wording will be available.                        |
| Run trial         | Not specified     | Friday        | None proposed        | Confirm who will run it.                                       |

**Revision reason:** P1 prevented invented commitments but gave no place for useful suggestions. P2 preserves that check and makes the origin of each proposed date visible. Tuesday is an invented suggestion, not a deadline from the brief. Keep P1 and the supplied comment identifiable; P2 has not received a new review.

**Alternative:** if a reviewer asks you to state Tuesday as agreed, keep it as a suggestion and explain the missing evidence. Agreeing with every comment is not the goal. Revise what the source and purpose support.

## Draft your own proposal

Use WF-LAB-01 v1, or a separate original invented workflow whose source you record. Keep the **first proposal**, any **revision** and the **reason** separate. The revision may keep the first proposal unchanged if you explain why the comment is unsupported. A short, checkable improvement is enough.

Describe the source and actual rights you have; if they are unclear, ask for the missing information or write a new invented example. Do not copy someone else's private work into this exercise. Choosing a display name or audience does not establish reuse rights.

**Self-check:** another learner can find the exact source, understand the problem, follow the proposed change, reproduce its stated check and see its limits. Your revision reason addresses the source or comment. Your audience plan is separate from submission or permission. This is your judgment, not an automatic grade.

You may keep the contribution private or cancel a future sharing plan while retaining the draft. The six-step learning record is complete without a contribution. Saving unfinished work is allowed; download a copy if browser saving fails. Use the same browser address to return to an explicit save.
