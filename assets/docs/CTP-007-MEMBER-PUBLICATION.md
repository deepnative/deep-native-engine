# Member contribution sharing

This bounded #97 implementation adds ordinary private proposal intake and a complete exact-revision editorial acceptance, owner sharing and withdrawal path. It does not publish the #93 foundation pack or complete the broader #40/#97/#122 acceptance.

`DNE_MEMBER_CONTRIBUTIONS=enabled` deliberately enables new ordinary intake, editorial acceptance and publication. The default is disabled. Hosted configuration permits the flag, but this change does not enable it, appoint staff or claim actual moderation coverage. A real moderation contact and current authorized staff are factual inputs before real shared-space use; no response SLA is promised. Existing invented sample intake remains available under its original attestation when ordinary creation is off.

A member opens **My private proposals**, saves their original text with source/rights notes and submits for private moderation. Submission grants no display permission. Existing current moderator/platform-admin authority permits **Accept this revision privately** after checking that exact revision and its notes. It is ordinary editorial review, never a credential or qualified assessment. The existing request-changes/correction/resubmission, quarantine and rejection flow remains intact.

The owner opens **Inspect editorial acceptance and sharing**. Accepted text remains private. Sharing requires choosing an attribution, the **Signed-in members only** audience, and a fresh unchecked `member-display-v1` permission to display that exact revision on this platform. The author retains ownership; the flow grants no anonymous/public distribution or external reuse rights. Other current members can then read the shared contribution with its chosen attribution, sources, revision and editorial label. Legacy sample provenance remains visible.

Migration 075 stores one owner-linked acceptance/publication record per proposal revision, with acting staff role/time internally and owner-facing permission/status timestamps. It never copies member text into `content_versions`: those curriculum records cannot satisfy member redaction. Every contribution read locks and validates the original proposal and its publication record. Changed source/revision/rights or quarantine, rejection and proposal withdrawal atomically withdraw display permission and remove attribution. Withdrawing only sharing keeps the private original. A withdrawn revision cannot be reactivated by a stale form or new request key; a new proposal is needed to share again. Account deletion cascades the source and its publication records. Previously obtained copies cannot be recalled.

Structured member export v27 appends `proposalPublications` after every existing section (index 46), preserving cursor positions. It contains the owner's proposal/revision, acceptance date, state, chosen attribution while retained, audience, permission version, publication and withdrawal dates. It excludes staff identities and operation references. Source text remains only in the existing owner-filtered proposal section; withdrawal continues to redact that section. Retained legacy sample attestations are not converted into ordinary or sharing consent.

Each publication request has a canonical operation reference. After an uncertain response, the page offers inspection and a deliberate repeat; nothing auto-retries. **Inspect an earlier publication request** obtains a fresh form after a restart, with the reference submitted in the request body. Creation-off leaves current-owner inspection, exact original-operation recovery, withdrawal and erasure available. A withdrawn or erased source cannot be recreated from a receipt. Current member/staff authority and deadlines cover database waits, COMMIT and response rendering.
