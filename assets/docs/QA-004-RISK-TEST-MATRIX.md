# QA-004: risk-based abuse and regression matrix

This is the executable-design handoff for [QA-004 #18](https://github.com/deepnative/deep-native-engine/issues/18) and [CTP-023 #42](https://github.com/deepnative/deep-native-engine/issues/42). Read it with the [threat model](QA-004-THREAT-MODEL.md), the [approved scenario register](../../tests/e2e/scenarios.json) and the [quality gates](context/QUALITY-GATES.md). **Existing** means a named synthetic local test already exercises the stated slice; **proposed** means the responsible feature issue must add and demonstrate it before its release gate. A proposed case is never counted in the current local-slice numerator. Every one of the 100 full-MVP cases in the 27 requirement families has a browser test and passed a formal release run on 11 November 2026 with invented data. On 11 November 2026 the owner accepted that coverage as demo evidence, with its stated limits ([record](https://github.com/deepnative/deep-native-engine/issues/94#issuecomment-6809159129)); it is not a release or launch approval, and the register entries keep their `outstanding` status. The [final state of every row](#final-state-of-every-row-11-november-2026) is recorded below.

For each new capability, its owner must attach an acceptance criterion, the relevant risk IDs, a first-failure reproduction, deterministic unit tests for decisions, real PostgreSQL integration for state/races, and a browser journey for observable authorization or recovery. External providers may be simulated only at their explicit adapter boundary. Use isolated members and databases, controlled clocks and barriers or observed `pg_blocking_pids` for races. A sleep, a mock of the application policy, or a successful retry alone does not prove the invariant. The new scenario ID enters the approved release denominator before claiming coverage; the full-MVP register stays visible until its criteria are decomposed and passed.

## Execution and acceptance order

1. A feature owner copies the relevant M cases into that issue's observable acceptance and test design **before coding**. Record who owns any provider, staff, rights, privacy or payment decision; an unresolved launch prerequisite stays explicit.
