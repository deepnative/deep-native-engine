# PLAN-003: proposed expert leads and evidence request

Research snapshot: **23 September 2026**. Supports [issue #13](https://github.com/deepnative/deep-native-engine/issues/13) and the [capacity and operations proposal](PLAN-003-CAPACITY-OPERATIONS.md). This is **public-source lead research**, not a vetted roster, endorsement, expression of interest, agreement, staffing reservation, or authorization to sell expert services. No outreach had been sent when this snapshot was prepared. All leads contribute **zero committed hours** to the [capacity workbook](PLAN-003-CAPACITY-MODEL.xlsx).

## Sourcing scope and service distinction

Deep Native Engine's shared foundation and participation path serves IT practitioners, people in other professions, and general learners without a paid coaching prerequisite. This search concerns only optional human coaching, pooled clinics, and qualified formal review. A general AI instructor may be suited to a beginner clinic but cannot thereby sign a technical, accounting, healthcare, legal, or other specialized assessment. An organization that offers training is a route to inquire about a provider, not a named expert. The initial geography for research is Ontario or remote delivery to Ontario; supported service geography and the first paid cohort's domain mix still need owner decisions.

The minimum sourcing lanes are:

| Lane | Possible service | Qualification evidence needed before assignment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation facilitator | Accessible AI orientation and pooled general clinics | Teaching example for beginner and noncoding learners, accessibility practice, safety/privacy handling, agreed clinic format and backup |
| Applied IT reviewer | Scoped software/AI workflow project review and technical coaching | Recent relevant work, review sample against a versioned rubric, security/privacy judgment, domain limits and signed human assessment responsibility |
| Non-IT domain reviewer | A named professional-domain project review where an offer promises one | Current domain qualification and independent verification where applicable, synthetic sample review, explicit limits against regulated advice outside the agreement |
| Service operations | Scheduling, acknowledgement, recovery, QA and support | Staffed coverage, escalation owner, time/cost evidence and backup; do not silently treat a facilitator as unlimited support |

## Evidence request and decision record

Use a stable provider ID and an access-controlled evidence location for any reply. Record the source, collection date, verifier and expiry/recheck date for each item. Do not upload prospect correspondence, credentials, contracts or member data into this public repository. A potential provider advances only through these states: `public lead` → `replied/interested` → `qualified for named service` → `terms agreed` → `dated committed capacity` → `owner approved for offer`. Skipping a state does not imply it was satisfied.

| Evidence to request or test | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- |
| Identity, role, domain and allowed service types | Named legal contracting party and named human deliverer; check public claims against supplied evidence and note specialties **not** covered. |
| Teaching or review capability | Review a synthetic beginner teaching outline or a synthetic project/artifact using the actual criteria; record accessibility, factual limits, feedback quality and who signs a formal assessment. |
| Privacy, security and conflicts | Agree to minimal data access, no training/reuse of member material, consent and deletion rules; declare conflicts and any subcontractor/AI use. Escalate regulated or client-confidential work rather than accepting it silently. |
| Availability and backup | Written dates, timezone, staffed windows, maximum individual and clinic hours, lead time, existing commitments, absence coverage and replacement rule for the proposed two-month pilot window. |
| Loaded cost and terms | Written CAD rate or quote **including** prep, notes, QA, cancellations, taxes/expenses as applicable; agreement, invoice method, response and recovery obligations. Public workshop prices are not hourly contractor quotes. |
| Owner decision | Record reviewer, decision/date, approved specialty/service/window, assigned backup or exception and the capacity calculation version. Reject or defer if any required lane is uncovered. |

The existing five-member workbook scenario needs **25 coach hours, 10 reviewer hours, 14.5 support hours and 4 analyst/QA hours across two months**. Those are workload estimates, not bookings. Split the 10 reviewer hours by actual promised specialty and cohort mix before checking qualified availability. Add a separately justified recovery and utilization reserve; the archived 80% booking figure is only an unapproved scenario. The scarcest qualified lane determines paid admission capacity. No prospect reply alone should change the workbook's readiness cell to `PASS`.

## Draft first inquiry (not sent)

> Subject: Exploring a scoped AI learning or project-review collaboration
>
> Hello [name/team],
>
> I’m working on Deep Native Engine, a learning and participation experience for people in IT, other professions and general AI learning. We are assessing **optional** human coaching and project review; no paid expert service is live or promised yet.
>
> Your public work on [specific sourced service] suggests a possible fit for [one named lane]. Would you be open to discussing a small, clearly scoped pilot service? We are evaluating [clinic/coaching/review] with stated preparation and feedback time, not an unlimited help desk or a request for regulated advice.
>
> If this is relevant, could you describe the work and domains you would personally cover, examples of recent teaching or review, a possible two-month availability window and timezone, your loaded CAD rate/quote, conflict and backup arrangements, and any privacy or contracting requirements? We would begin with synthetic examples and share no member or client material at this stage.
>
> A short reply about fit is enough. We can then discuss a precise brief and terms before either side makes a commitment.
>
> Thank you, [sender name and business contact]

Personalize the first line to the actual service and route through a public business channel. Do not imply an existing partnership, urgency, a job offer, funding, client volume or a guaranteed contract. One initial message per selected lead; any follow-up cadence and retention policy require an owner decision. Record exact sent text, date, channel, recipient business route and response privately, then update the issue with a non-sensitive status count.

## Owner decisions before contact or offer

The owner needs to choose the first paid cohort's geography and supported specialties, which of these leads (if any) to approach, a maximum number of initial contacts and channel/sender identity, an outreach follow-up/retention rule, and whether the draft service procedures in the [operations proposal](PLAN-003-CAPACITY-OPERATIONS.md) are approved or need revision. Contact permission does not approve hiring, expert access to member data, a price, a paid offer, or launch. Until verified contracts and dated hours meet the workload by specialty, PLAN-003's live-capacity blocker remains.
