# Expanded audience and participation journeys

Planning revision: ecosystem-v1, 21 September 2026. These are acceptance scenarios to add to the QA-002 register, not executed tests or achieved coverage. Read [the product direction](../PRODUCT-DIRECTION.md) first.

The complete release register must include the nine original roadmap journeys, build-prompt recovery/late-return/commercial-truth cases, and these additions. Map overlap explicitly without dropping requirements. Only the approved versioned register defines the denominator; this short list alone cannot establish full-MVP coverage. Unimplemented scenarios remain uncovered. Slice coverage and full-release coverage stay separate.

| ID | Actor and starting state | Action and observable outcome | Issues | Critical aspects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ECO-01 | General learner with no job title, client, contract or coding experience | Register with an exploratory goal, enter the foundation, complete a noncoding exercise and see private progress; no professional-coaching purchase or IT-fit approval is required | CTP-003, CTP-008, CTP-009 | Identity, consent and private progress |
| ECO-02 | IT practitioner and non-IT professional with different goals | Each selects a suitable path, opens accessible material and completes a relevant assignment; IT coding tasks are not imposed on noncoding paths | PLAN-004, CTP-007, CTP-009, QA-005 | Equivalent access and truthful readiness |
| ECO-03 | Member changes interests or returns after a gap | Update goals and resume; preserved evidence remains versioned, next actions reflect eligible content, and empty/error/recovery states stay useful | CTP-008, CTP-009, CTP-018 | Existing ownership and version integrity |
| ECO-04 | Member with private work joins a learning circle | Opt into a suitable group, participate within its rules, report a problem and leave; private evidence is not disclosed, revoked access is enforced, and moderation is accountable | CTP-005, CTP-020, CTP-021, QA-004 | 100% privacy, revocation and moderation-authorization cases |
| ECO-05 | Member proposes a reusable workflow or learning resource | Provide attribution/rights, submit for review, publish only after explicit consent and approval, then revoke sharing; unreviewed contributions cannot masquerade as qualified instruction | CTP-006, CTP-007, CTP-013, CTP-021 | 100% consent, content-access and revocation cases |
| ECO-06 | Learner wants study help rather than an interview or contract | Use source-grounded study/practice, choose a goal-appropriate format and see labelled feedback; career practice remains optional and unconfigured providers remain disabled | CTP-014, CTP-015, CTP-018 | 100% AI consent, budget and failure cases |
| ECO-07 | Member considers an optional coaching offer | See actual scope/capacity, accept the versioned offer explicitly, receive correct allowances and cancellation/export rights; no automatic upgrade or confusion between background and paid tier | CTP-002, CTP-011, CTP-012, CTP-016, CTP-017 | 100% payment, date, entitlement and concurrency cases |
| ECO-08 | Operator reviews learner/community outcomes and paid-service outcomes | View separate, defined measures for learning, participation, reviewed contributions and paid conversion; demo data/self-reported outcomes remain labelled and sensitive member data is not exposed | CTP-022, QA-004 | 100% staff authorization and privacy cases |

Before implementation, QA-002 maps each scenario to preconditions, actual UI/server/database steps, observable assertions, browser test IDs, the approved release scope and critical subcases. Cover non-IT and exploratory learners as well as technical/professional paths in fixtures. Every critical subcase requires 100% coverage; every required executed test must pass. Existing money/date/isolation/deletion tests remain required for their applicable optional service paths.
