# Evidence, assumptions, and launch decisions

**Version 1.0 · 15 September 2026 · CAD before applicable taxes**

This register distinguishes verified context, proposed business choices, and decisions requiring the actual owner or qualified specialist. It supports implementation without treating a working prototype as a validated business or an approved commercial launch.

## 1. Confirmed scope

- Individual independent IT contractors first, including individuals who pay through their company.
- All IT roles can enter the platform from the beginning. Each role/domain displays **available**, **limited coverage**, **in preparation**, or **retired** accurately.
- Target annual spend is CAD 10k to low six figures **per contractor**. The proposed CAD 12k/24k/48k/120k packages remain pricing hypotheses.
- Services include AI and domain training, study materials, coaching, interview practice, assessed projects, agent workflows, and career/delivery support.
- Canada-oriented commercial preparation and English-first content are working assumptions. Remote access does not imply every country, timezone, or specialty is supported immediately.

## 2. What the evidence establishes

**Relevant activity exists.** Statistics Canada reports meaningful but uneven AI adoption among self-employed people. Its population includes occupations outside IT. MBO reports continuing learning interest among US independents. Neither establishes Canadian purchasing intent at our prices. [S01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260730/dq260730b-eng.htm), [S03](https://www.mbopartners.com/state-of-independence/2025-ai-report)

**The features face established competition.** Current vendors already combine learning materials, practice, AI assistance, and coaching. Integrated professional assignments, credible review, domain relevance, and maintained tools are proposed differentiation to test. Competitor presence is not premium-price evidence. [S04](https://www.pluralsight.com/individuals/pricing), [S05](https://www.tryexponent.com/upgrade), [S06](https://interviewkickstart.com/guide/interview-kickstart-cost)

**Validation remains outstanding.** Paid pilots, continuation, actual service costs, expert coverage, and useful repeat usage must be observed. No total market estimate, proven earnings uplift, guaranteed placement, or CAD 120k willingness-to-pay claim is established.

## 4. Practical Canadian operating controls

### Member and client data

Use synthetic cases by default. Accept client material only within documented rights and permitted purpose; a member's upload does not erase client confidentiality restrictions. Separate learning consent, recording consent, marketing consent, and permission to reuse work publicly. Keep portfolios private until explicitly shared.

Implement scoped member/coach/admin access, secure storage, expiring share links, recording choice, export/deletion requests, retention rules, and an incident procedure. Financial-record retention may continue separately after learning data is deleted. Check AI-provider terms/settings before promising no training use or Canadian residency. Cross-border processing is not categorically forbidden under PIPEDA, but accountability remains with the organization. Applicability depends on provincial and cross-border facts. [OPC scope](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/information-and-advice-for-individuals/your-privacy-rights/businesses-and-your-personal-information/), [transfers](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/airports-and-borders/gl_dab_090127/), [AI principles](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/technology/artificial-intelligence/gd_principles_ai/)

### Commercial messages

Maintain the applicable consent or other valid basis, date/source, sender details, and suppression history. Do not assume a publicly visible address permits outreach. Commercial electronic messages generally require consent, identification, and unsubscribe handling; implement immediate suppression, within CRTC's maximum ten-business-day window. AI may prepare drafts; campaign sending requires an authorized, reviewable recipient basis. [S10](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm)

### Tax, invoices, and training classification

Confirm entity, registration, supply type, and customer location before real billing. The usual GST/HST small-supplier threshold is CAD30k, with different single-quarter and rolling-four-quarter timing tests. [Registration](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) Training, coaching, and subscription access do not automatically share an exemption. [Classification](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/20-4/vocational-schools-courses.html) Keep tax outside revenue and support clear invoice identity, service period, description, payment terms, tax amount/status, and registration information. [Invoices](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html)

After identifying the operating province, check whether the actual offer is professional development or a regulated vocational program. Ontario's official guidance describes exemptions, including some same-vocation upgrading, and prescreening. This is an example requiring assessment, not a conclusion that Ontario applies or that the platform qualifies. Do not advertise accreditation, government approval, guaranteed tax deductions, or grant eligibility without evidence. [S15](https://www.ontario.ca/page/exemptions-registration-career-colleges)

## 6. Evidence labels to preserve

Use **verified source context**, **planning assumption**, **operator decision**, **member self-report**, or **verified customer outcome** as appropriate. Record date, source, reviewer, and limitation. Software completion, generated testimonials, simulated payments, and demo assessments never count as real customer evidence.
