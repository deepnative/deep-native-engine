# Contractor Success Platform

## Business plan and Codex implementation package

Prepared 15 September 2026. All proposed prices and model amounts are in Canadian dollars, before applicable taxes.

### Confirmed direction

- The customers are individual independent IT contractors, including individuals operating through their own corporations.
- The platform welcomes all IT contractor roles from the beginning.
- The target annual spend is approximately CAD 10,000 through the low six figures **per contractor**.
- Services combine AI training, domain learning, study materials, coaching, mock interviews, assessed practical projects, maintained agent workflows, and support for professional growth.
- This package defines the business and gives Codex actionable instructions to build the first platform release.

### Read and use

1. Read **01-business-plan.md** for positioning, services, annual subscriptions, operating policies, acquisition, economics, risks, and launch decisions.
2. Paste the entire contents of **02-codex-build-prompt.md** into a Codex task in your chosen project. It is a self-contained implementation brief. Attach this whole folder when possible.
3. Use **03-roadmap-and-backlog.md** for milestones, issue dependencies, acceptance criteria, and the evidence required to mark work complete.
4. Open **04-financial-model.xlsx** to change acquisition, conversion, delivery, and payment assumptions. The scenario results are planning calculations, not revenue forecasts supported by sales.
5. Read **05-evidence-and-decisions.md** for current sources, research limitations, unconfirmed business choices, and launch checks.

### Commercial direction

The proposed annual ladder is CAD 12,000, CAD 24,000, CAD 48,000, and CAD 120,000. Launch the CAD 12,000 program through a CAD 3,000 paid pilot. Higher tiers remain unpublished or inquiry-only until expert capacity, customer benefit, and delivery economics are demonstrated. The CAD 120,000 tier is a substantial managed service for an established solo consulting practice. Its feasibility is unvalidated.

The platform can admit contractors across roles while displaying the actual availability of role-specific lessons, coaches, and domain reviews. Broad access does not imply that every specialty is fully staffed or that an unpublished course exists.

### Execution boundary

The initial Codex assignment is to implement and verify the private MVP, using synthetic examples and clearly labelled simulated integrations when credentials are absent. It also prepares release and paid-pilot launch materials. This package does not itself authorize charging cards, contacting prospects, using client-confidential information, buying services, or publishing a live commercial offer. Routine local implementation and testing proceed without repeated confirmation.

### Status

Business design and implementation specification. No customer interviews, paid pilots, renewals, revenue, expert appointments, or platform implementation are claimed by this package. Company name, operating province, founder time budget, startup capital, confirmed experts, and acquisition network still require real information. Default choices are documented so development can proceed.

### Package verification

Prices, human-service allowances, pilot conversion dates, and tier names were reconciled across the documents. The backlog contains 25 implementation issues. Financial scenarios were independently recalculated; changing the scenario and a later-month input updated the workbook, and exported sheets contained no formula-error cells. All five workbook sheets were visually reviewed. The native Microsoft Excel application was not used for this verification.

The base financial case is deliberately candid: about CAD 160k of first-year earned service revenue accompanies about CAD 60k of modeled operating loss. The upper subscription tiers are not required to make that scenario's revenue. Read the expense assumptions and prepaid-service reserve before choosing a launch budget.
