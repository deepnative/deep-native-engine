# Protect people and their work

**Goal:** decide what information may be used in a learning exercise and where a draft may be shared. **Suggested time:** 15 minutes.

Before putting information into a tool, ask: **Do I have permission to use it here? Does this task need the real detail? Where could the output go?** Names, private messages, client material, unpublished work, credentials and health or financial details can create risk even when a task sounds routine. In this local preview, use invented or sample information only. Do not paste real secrets or another person's private work.

Input permission and output sharing are different decisions. A document you can read may not be yours to upload, adapt or publish. A private learning artifact does not become public because you join a circle or ask for feedback. Sharing requires a chosen destination, rights/attribution check and a way to stop or revise the sharing. A generated passage also needs checking before you claim authorship or accuracy.

## Practise a permission decision

For each case, choose **use synthetic**, **seek permission**, or **stop**. Explain one sentence.

- **Exploratory:** You want help writing a neighbourhood-event message, but the volunteer list contains real phone numbers. Create invented names and leave contact details out.
- **Non-IT professional:** A colleague's real meeting notes include a client complaint. Seek permission and use a synthetic version for learning; do not upload the real notes into an unapproved tool.
- **IT practitioner:** A private repository has a production API key and customer logs. Stop the upload. Build a minimal invented test fixture that still shows the sign-up failure path.

**Self-check:** Your sample contains no real identifiers, credentials or copyrighted excerpt you lack rights to use. Your proposed sharing scope names the audience and purpose. If any answer is uncertain, pause and ask the accountable owner; “the AI needs it” is not permission.

The examples above teach a habit. They do not assert that the current application has live consent, moderation, retention or legal-compliance controls.
