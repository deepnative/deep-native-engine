# What stays when a member deletes their account

Deleting an account (My account → Delete your account) deletes the member's principal row. Everything else goes through `ON DELETE CASCADE` foreign keys that lead back to it: the learner profile, workspace, saved work, evidence and its derived records, sharing choices, export links, sign-in records and the rest. Stored files are removed before the row goes (`src/deletion-recovery.ts`), and a durable marker resumes a deletion that stopped part-way.

This page lists every table whose rows **don't** go with the account, and every member link in those rows. `tests/integration/member-erasure-coverage.test.ts` reads both tables below and compares them with the database catalog after all migrations:

- **ERASE-01/02:** a table that isn't removed through a cascade, or a column named `member_id`, `learner_id`, `owner_id`, `owner_principal_id`, `workspace_id` or `principal_id` without a cascading key, fails the test until it is listed here with a reason. An entry that no longer applies fails it too.
- **ERASE-03/04:** after a seeded member is deleted, a scan of every text, ID and JSON column finds their account ID, workspace ID, email fingerprint, display name and private note only in the rows marked "Kept" below.

The account page tells members the same thing in plain words before they delete (ERASE-06, browser scenario L318), and `/terms` shows the same list (RETAIN-01/02).

## Billing records: six years (#12 D08, owner decision 10 November 2026)

Issued invoices and the payments and refunds recorded against them are kept after the account is deleted, following Canada Revenue Agency record-keeping rules: **until 31 December six years after the invoice's tax year**, then removed automatically. This is the owner's decision for the demo, not legal advice, and still needs qualified review before launch. Everything else goes as described on this page; backups expire within 30 days. The period is defined once in `src/retention.ts`, and the account page, the response to a deletion (RETAIN-03) and `/terms` all show it. The deletion response names both exceptions and their expiry and doesn't claim everything is erased (full-MVP cases F-BUILD-08-D and F-ROADMAP-09-D in `tests/full-e2e/build-08-d.spec.ts` and `tests/full-e2e/roadmap-09-d.spec.ts`).

What a kept invoice holds was checked on 10 November 2026 against `src/simple-invoice-store.ts`: the invoice number and date, the issuer, the billing name, optional company and email, the line items (course or coaching descriptions, coaching minutes and rate version), amounts, tax and payment instructions, any void reason, and the recorded payments and refunds (amount, date, kind, staff evidence reference). It holds no learning records, saved work, profile or sign-in data, and the link to the account (`owner_id`) is cleared. The course agreement behind it (`simple_invoice_plans`), coaching months, coaching terms and the billing profile are deleted with the account. Sample invoices are deleted.

Removal (#12 D08, owner decision 10 November 2026): Deep Native Inc.'s tax year ends on 31 December. A kept invoice and its payment records stay until 31 December six years after the invoice's tax year (a 2026 invoice stays until 31 December 2032), then the running service removes them automatically. It checks a minute after start and then every six hours. Migration 136 adds `simple_invoices.owner_deleted_at`, the time the account link was cleared; invoices kept before 10 November have no recorded time. The database refuses to delete a kept invoice before its date, and never deletes an invoice whose account still exists. Each removal leaves one row in `simple_invoice_retention_removals` with the tax year, the date, the deletion time and the number of payment records, and no name, email, number or amount. The response after deleting an account shows that account's own date (RETEXP-01..04).

## Not covered here

- Hosted object storage, provider copies and database backups. The owner set database backup retention to 30 days on 9 November 2026 (#12, D08); copies in backups go when those backups expire. The account page, the response to a deletion and `/terms` say "up to 30 days".
- A deleted member's ID inside another member's own records, such as a reply to their post. Those follow each feature's own rules.
- Staff accounts (`principals.kind='staff'`) are a separate kind and aren't deleted through this path.
