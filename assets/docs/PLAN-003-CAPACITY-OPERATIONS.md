## Scope and source precedence

The [current product direction](PRODUCT-DIRECTION.md) makes learning and participation common to IT practitioners, other professionals, and general learners. Tailored coaching and formal expert review are optional, capacity-gated services. A learner's background or goal does not create an expert entitlement, paid plan, or staff role. The [PLAN-001 reconciliation](PLAN-001-SCOPE-RECONCILIATION.md) identifies an interim content owner but no named, qualified domain-review pool.

The preserved [roadmap](context/source-2026-09-15/outputs/contractor-platform/03-roadmap-and-backlog.md) defines the historical standalone two-month pilot allowances and four pooled monthly clinics. The preserved [business plan](context/source-2026-09-15/outputs/contractor-platform/01-business-plan.md) supplies draft booking rules and *unverified* loaded hourly cost assumptions. These sources apply only to the optional coached offer where consistent with current direction. No source identifies committed experts or their current rates. This plan does not set foundation pricing or membership access.

## Capacity model and synthetic allocation

The workbook has an editable `Capacity` sheet and an empty `Roster` evidence sheet. Blue input cells distinguish source allowances from illustrative workload assumptions. Cost rates are carried from the archived business plan solely for scenario calculation; replace them only with supported quotes or agreements. A committed expert is counted only when the roster has qualification evidence, a signed agreement, a dated availability window, a loaded rate, conflict review, and a backup or recorded exception. The workbook's readiness cell stays **UNCONFIRMED** until these prerequisites are reviewed manually. A numerical surplus alone never proves service readiness.

The sample cohort is **five synthetic pilot members, two billing months, one clinic group**. Each member consumes two 60-minute coaching units (45 minutes contact and 15 minutes preparation/notes), four 30-minute reviews (about 20 minutes review and 10 minutes preparation), two monthly 60-minute support allocations, and an internal one-time onboarding reserve of 60 coach plus 30 support minutes. That is 3 coach, 2 reviewer, and 2.5 support hours per member, or **37.5 individual staff hours** for five. Onboarding is an internal reserve, not a third purchased coaching call. Baseline and closeout fit these funded units; no extra sessions are assumed.

Four one-hour clinics per month for two months mean **eight group-contact hours**, independent of whether five or sixteen members attend. For the example only, each clinic also reserves 15 minutes each for preparation, notes, QA, and scheduling slack: eight additional staff hours. Contact and preparation are assigned to coach, notes and QA to analyst, and scheduling to support. The example therefore totals **53.5 staff hours** across two months: coach 25, reviewer 10, support 14.5, analyst 4. These supplementary minutes are new planning assumptions, **not** a promised service level or a verified labor estimate. The workbook allocates each minute to one activity and one role. It excludes content maintenance, hiring, cancellation/recovery load, management overhead, and other cohorts; those need separate reserves before an offer can be priced or sold.

The clinic capacity boundary is per group. Five through sixteen members use one group and eight clinics across this example period; seventeen require two groups and sixteen clinics, subject to qualified facilitator and support availability. Do not multiply pooled clinic hours by members. Do not count a clinic as an individual coaching/review unit. For any real cohort, allocate specialties, time zones, accessibility needs, service windows, and overlapping commitments by named expert before accepting a booking. The scarcest qualified role and specialty limits the admitted cohort. An unmatched specialty is shown as a gap; it does not block the shared foundation or non-expert participation.

## Roster evidence and admission gate

Each potential provider receives a stable roster ID. A **proposed** contact is an unverified lead and contributes zero committed hours. Record role and supported domain, service types, credential/work evidence with source and verifier, exact availability dates/hours and time zone, loaded cost with quote/agreement evidence, conflict-of-interest decision, backup provider or exception, and the signed terms date. Record consent and limits for use of their profile. Avoid private personal details in this repository; use an access-controlled evidence location and reference ID. The blank roster is intentional: no candidate has been presented or verified.

Before describing expert service as available or taking a paid booking, the owner must:

1. Verify the specific provider's expertise for the domain and service, agreement, conflicts, and backup coverage; document the reviewer and date.
2. Map qualified hours to the actual pilot window, existing commitments, prep/notes, QA, support, slack, and a recovery reserve. Treat a verbal estimate or a proposed contact as zero committed hours.
3. Check the minimum of every required specialty and role against the booked load, plus a defensible utilization cap. The historic finance draft used 80% productive-hour booking as a scenario assumption; no actual utilization limit is approved here.
4. Have the owner approve the exact offer, staffed window, service terms, and price with applicable review. If any gate fails, show a later start or a service without that expert feature; never silently substitute AI feedback for qualified formal review.

The current gate is **not met**: there are no verified providers, signed terms, cost quotes, or dated committed hours in the source package. Owner review of this procedure is also pending. This is a launch/paid-service blocker, not evidence that common learning must wait.

## Proposed operating rules for owner review

These are draft policies derived from the archived offer and require owner, operations, and applicable terms review before use with members.

| Topic | Proposed rule | Required operating record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service scope | Show the exact unit duration, contact/preparation split, deliverable, artifact size and up to three review criteria before booking. A resubmission uses another unit unless the accepted terms say otherwise. | Versioned offer, booking, scoped artifact and accepted terms |
| Turnaround | Show real qualified availability before booking. Proposed Professional support acknowledgement target is two business days during staffed hours; acknowledgement is not resolution. Set a separate realistic review completion date at booking. | Request timestamp, acknowledgement, accepted due date, resolution and exception |
| Staffed window | Default proposal: Monday-Friday, 09:00-17:00 Eastern, excluding a published holiday calendar. Show the member-local equivalent and the actual expert's available slots. No 24-hour coverage is implied. | Published calendar/version, member timezone, provider slots |
| Rescheduling | Proposed member window: at least 24 hours before the session. Present cutoff and local time at confirmation. Exceptions require a recorded operator decision. | Notice time, reason, decision and credit change |
| No-show | A credit may be consumed only under the version of the policy the member accepted, after a documented no-show and exception review. Never infer acceptance from this draft. | Attendance evidence, accepted policy version, operator decision |
| Provider cancellation/unavailability | Restore the relevant unit and offer dated replacement qualified availability. Do not erase the replacement with ordinary period expiry. If replacement is impossible, escalate to a documented service-recovery decision. | Cause, restored credit, offered slots, member decision and closure |
| Service quality and privacy | Human reviewer signs formal feedback; AI assistance is labelled. Escalate conflict, disputed feedback, privacy or accessibility problems to a named operator. Recording requires applicable participant consent; offer a written summary if unsuitable. | Reviewer identity, escalation, consent choice, corrective action |

The owner must decide the review turnaround by service/specialty, holiday calendar, no-show exceptions and evidence standard, expiry/recovery window, backup exception threshold, utilization/recovery reserve, supported domains and launch geography, and any jurisdiction-specific terms. Do not publish the archived coaching prices or these rules as accepted current terms before that review.
