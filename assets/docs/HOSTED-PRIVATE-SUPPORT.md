# Private support intake and assignment

Issue [#195](https://github.com/deepnative/deep-native-engine/issues/195) extends the delivered [support conversation](PRIVATE-SAMPLE-SUPPORT.md) and [exact operator assignment](PRIVATE-SUPPORT-ASSIGNMENT.md). Every member can send an own private request when `DNE_PRIVATE_SUPPORT_INTAKE=enabled`. The setting defaults to disabled and is compatible with the authorized Azure hosting configuration; it does not activate a live provider, payment or staffed SLA.

## Runnable journey

1. From the learning space, open **Private support**, then **New private request**. Enter a subject and request, and explicitly confirm sending without a guaranteed response time. This form does not require an invented-information claim, a paid plan or a test-minute allowance.
2. Inspect the private receipt. Received, acknowledgement and resolution are distinct facts. Sending never assigns an operator, acknowledges work or promises a response deadline.
3. A current administrator signs in through the existing staff form, opens **Support request assignments**, then **Open support intake inbox**. The inbox contains only receipt ID, received time and acknowledgement/resolution/withdrawal timestamps. It never selects or renders subject, body, notes or member identity. It shows at most 20 receipts with signed administrator-bound continuation. Each page rechecks current authority; deleting accounts and inactive owners are excluded.
4. The intended operator obtains **My local assignment ID** from their own staff tools. The administrator enters that noncredential reference on the selected inbox receipt, checks the exact references and explicitly confirms a finite UTC access window. Existing grant checks reject stale roles, credentials, requests and excessive expiry. Discovery and checking do not create access.
5. The operator opens **Your granted support requests**, separately acknowledges, saves an internal note if needed, and deliberately sends a member-visible reply. Internal notes never enter member pages or exports. A reply does not resolve the request; resolution is another explicit action and does not backfill acknowledgement.
6. The member reloads the receipt and can download their request and replies with the generic attribution **Support operator**. No staff identity or internal note is exported. The existing test-minute tools are separate; ordinary private intake does not show an allocation form unless there is an existing receipt to inspect.
7. Administrators can revoke an exact grant. Current authority is checked on future reads and writes. Members can withdraw request text and all messages, retaining only a content-free lifecycle receipt; deleting the account removes its request and inbox receipt. Previously downloaded copies and backup retention remain separate from application erasure.

The existing `DNE_LOCAL_STAFF_ENTRY=enabled` and `DNE_LOCAL_SUPPORT_ASSIGNMENT=enabled` settings gate staff entry and assignment/inbox administration. Their historical names do not make them staff provisioning tools. A credential, a role and a deliberate exact finite grant remain separate prerequisites for operator access. Do not renew expired hosted identities silently or treat a local synthetic credential as a real staffing commitment.

## Retention, rollback and staffing

Requests and visible replies remain until member withdrawal or account deletion. Internal notes follow the same request lifecycle and never become member-visible by resolving. Existing bounded histories, exact mutation keys, uncertain-result inspection and terminal-state rules are unchanged. No new database migration, automatic grant, notification, entitlement, quota, business-hours clock or service obligation is introduced.

Disable `DNE_PRIVATE_SUPPORT_INTAKE` to return new intake to the legacy invented-data form. The ordinary private form is rejected after that configuration change, but retained private receipts, inspection, export, withdrawal and account deletion continue. Generic receipt/operator labels remain truthful after rollback. Disable support assignment administration to remove the inbox and new administrator assignment surface; inspect existing exact grants through the documented controlled recovery path instead of dropping records. Do not restore withdrawn text.

Initial support has no published response deadline, paid allowance or guaranteed staffed hours. The owner must name the actual hosted operator before a hosted staffing demonstration or coverage claim. This implementation does not provision or renew hosted identities, send messages or establish support availability. Historical demands for a two-business-day promise and an approved paid schedule are not prerequisites for the no-charge intake feature.
